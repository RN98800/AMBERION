About

Engineering with purpose.

From design intent to built reality. AMBERION brings together engineering, digital technology, sustainability and execution to create MEP systems that perform — technically, commercially and over the life of the building.

Who we are

Developer mindset.
Engineering excellence.

AMBERION is an MEP engineering and contracting firm headquartered in Bengaluru, built on a simple belief: better contracting starts with better understanding, and better design starts with knowing how it will be built.

Our leadership brings over five decades of combined MEP experience across developer organisations, design consultancies and project execution. This experience spans large-scale residential, commercial and institutional developments across Bengaluru, Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad, NCR and Dubai.

We bring together the perspectives of the developer, designer and contractor — connecting design intent, commercial priorities, constructability and site realities.

We partner with developers, architects and institutions to take projects from design intent to built reality, combining engineering excellence, sustainability, digital innovation and disciplined execution.

Vision

To be the trusted MEP partner for creating sustainable, resilient and high-performance built environments.

Mission

AMBERION delivers integrated MEP solutions from concept to commissioning, blending disciplined engineering with sustainable practices and intelligent technology. We partner with developers, architects and institutions to create efficient, resilient and high-performing buildings through integrated design and execution.
