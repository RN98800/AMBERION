01 — Design consultancy

Engineering the design so it can be built.

Integrated MEP engineering from concept through construction administration, supported by BIM, digital coordination and advanced engineering tools.

  • Concept & schematic design

    System selection, load assessment, space planning for services and early coordination with architecture and structure.

  • Detailed design & documentation

    Design development, calculations, specifications, schedules and tender-ready drawing sets across all MEP disciplines.

  • BIM & digital engineering

    Modelling, clash detection and services coordination so issues are resolved in the model rather than on site. A capability within design consultancy, applied across every pillar.

  • Tender & procurement support

    Bill of quantities, tender documentation, technical evaluation and vendor assessment support.

  • Construction administration

    Shop drawing review, site queries, design intent verification and support through installation and commissioning.

02 — Contracting

Execution led by engineering.

Engineering-led execution covering project management, procurement, installation, QA/QC, testing, commissioning and timely handover, with a focus on value and optimised cost.

  • Project management

    Programme planning, resource management, interface coordination and progress control against milestones.

  • Procurement

    Vendor selection, technical alignment, expediting and material management focused on value rather than lowest cost alone.

  • Installation

    Disciplined site execution across PHE, HVAC, electrical, fire protection, fire alarm, ELV and security systems.

  • QA / QC

    Inspection regimes, method statements, quality plans and non-conformance control through the installation programme.

  • Testing, commissioning & handover

    Systematic testing, balancing, integrated commissioning, documentation, O&M handover and client training.

03 — Energy & sustainability

Performance by design.

Energy-efficient engineering, green building strategies, IGBC and USGBC / LEED support, existing-building BEE energy auditing and energy optimisation.

  • Energy-efficient engineering

    System-level efficiency built into design decisions, from equipment selection to control strategy.

  • Green building strategies

    Sustainable MEP strategies aligned to project goals, applied from concept rather than retro-fitted at certification stage.

  • IGBC and USGBC / LEED support

    Documentation and engineering support for green building rating requirements.

  • Existing-building energy auditing

    BEE energy auditing for existing buildings, identifying consumption patterns and improvement opportunities.

  • Energy optimisation

    Measures to reduce consumption and operating cost while protecting occupant comfort and system reliability.

AMBERION does not claim BEE accreditation or empanelment, certification-body status or proprietary AI technology. Any accreditation claims will be published only once formally confirmed.
